# Completeness checks by document type

What to look for before marking an item `received`. These are practice-derived checks for completeness, not rules for acceptance: the professional and the firm's lender, investor or regulator decide what is acceptable. Look-back periods marked (CFPB) come from the CFPB loan application packet; a lender may ask for more.

## Contents

- Checks for every document
- Tax documents
- Income documents
- Bank and asset statements
- Identity documents
- Signed documents
- Gift documentation
- Business records

## Checks for every document

| Check | Reject when |
|---|---|
| Right person | The name on the document is not the client, co-client or their business |
| Right period | The tax year, statement month or date is not the one requested |
| Complete | Pages are missing ("page 2 of 5" with fewer pages present), or the image is cropped |
| Legible | Names, dates or key fields cannot be read |
| Right document | It is a different form from the one requested (a pay stub sent for a W-2) |

A name that differs slightly (maiden name, middle initial) is not a rejection: list it under "For you to confirm".

## Tax documents

| Document | Check |
|---|---|
| Form W-2 | Tax year in the form heading; employee name; one per employer the client named |
| Forms 1099 (INT, DIV, NEC, MISC, K, G, R) | Tax year; recipient name; the right form type for the item |
| Brokerage 1099 | The consolidated year-end statement, all pages, not a monthly statement. Note if it is marked "corrected" or "preliminary" |
| Form SSA-1099 | Tax year; beneficiary name |
| Form 1095-A | Coverage year; covers the months the client had Marketplace insurance |
| Form 1098 (mortgage interest) | Tax year; borrower name; property matches |
| Prior-year return | Complete return with all schedules, not only the first two pages |
| Charitable receipts | Organization name, date and amount visible |

## Income documents

| Document | Check |
|---|---|
| Pay stubs | Cover the last 30 days (CFPB); show employer, employee name, pay period and year-to-date totals; consecutive, with no gap |
| W-2s for a loan | The last two years (CFPB), one per employer |
| Federal tax returns for a loan | The last two years (CFPB), all pages and schedules, signed |
| Other income | Award letter or statement names the client and shows the current amount |

## Bank and asset statements

| Check | Detail |
|---|---|
| Two most recent statements (CFPB) | Consecutive months, the latest being the most recent one issued |
| All pages | Every numbered page, including blank and disclosure pages |
| Account holder | Name visible and matches the client |
| Statement, not a transaction list | An official statement with the institution's name, account holder and period. A screenshot of an app balance is not one |
| Down payment source | Statements show at least two months' history of ownership (CFPB) |

Large or unusual deposits are for the loan officer to judge. Do not flag or question them to the client; mention them under "For you to confirm" only if the user asked for that.

## Identity documents

| Check | Detail |
|---|---|
| Unexpired | Expiry date is in the future |
| Whole document | Front and, where the firm asks for it, back; all four corners visible |
| Legible photo and name | Not obscured by glare or a finger |

Never transcribe the document number.

## Signed documents

| Document | Check |
|---|---|
| Engagement letter, fee agreement, statement of work | Signed and dated by every client named in it; every page present |
| Tax return copy for a lender | Signature page present |
| E-file authorization | Signed and dated by each taxpayer; the professional confirms the signature method is acceptable |

## Gift documentation

A gift letter for a Fannie Mae loan must be signed by the donor and state the actual or maximum amount, that no repayment is expected, and the donor's name, address, telephone number and relationship to the borrower (Selling Guide B3-4.3-04). Reject a letter missing any of these, naming the missing element. Transfer evidence is separate: a donor's check or withdrawal slip with the borrower's deposit slip, or evidence of an electronic transfer.

## Business records

| Document | Check |
|---|---|
| Bank and card statements | Every month in the requested range, no gaps |
| Profit and loss statement | Covers the stated period; names the business |
| Form W-9 | Signed and dated; name and taxpayer classification completed |
| Loan statements | Most recent; shows balance and lender |
